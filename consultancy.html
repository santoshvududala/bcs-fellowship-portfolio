Consultancy

Advising clients and transforming enterprise technology through quality engineering, automation and data expertise

SITUATION · WHAT

18+ Years of Technology Consultancy

Across 18+ years in IT, I have worked in client-facing technology and quality leadership roles across AIG, Bank of America and TIAA. My consultancy experience spans quality engineering, intelligent automation, GenAI, ETL, data engineering, AML, cloud migration and enterprise applications.

The common challenge across these engagements has been translating complex business and technology requirements into reliable, scalable solutions while improving quality, data integrity, delivery efficiency and stakeholder confidence.

TASK · WHY

Advise Organisations on the Effective Use of Technology

My role has been to advise and assist client organisations in the deployment, use and development of technology. This has included defining QA and automation strategies, selecting validation approaches, designing scalable frameworks, advising business and technical stakeholders, coordinating UAT and helping teams adopt new technologies successfully.

This is distinct from my Responsibility role: Consultancy focuses on the technology advice and transformation delivered across client engagements, while Responsibility focuses on the internal technical authority and release accountability I hold within AIG.

ACTION · HOW

Client Advisory and Transformation Delivery

AIG — GenAI Quality Engineering

I advised and led quality engineering for the GenAI-powered Claim Assist platform, designing validation across APIs, UI, AI-model behaviour, CI/CD and GenAI document extraction. I validated 70–80+ data fields, supported claim workflows and helped QA and business teams understand GenAI testing and model-validation practices.

Bank of America — AML & IICS Transformation

For the MANTAS AML platform and PowerCenter-to-IICS migration, I advised on Tosca-based controls automation and validated 300+ workflows, including mappings, connections, data types, configurations and end-to-end transformations. I partnered with product owners and business users through UAT validation and sign-off.

TIAA — Enterprise Data & ETL

I led enterprise automation and ETL initiatives, coordinating QA across 10 downstream applications, millions of records and 150+ tables. I advised delivery teams on data-validation approaches, automation and structured stakeholder reporting for financial reporting and transformation programs.

Capability & Adoption

Across engagements, I translated specialist QA, automation and data-engineering knowledge into reusable practices, training and stakeholder guidance so client teams could adopt technology with stronger controls and greater confidence.
