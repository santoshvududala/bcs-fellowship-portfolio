01 · RESPONSIBILITY

Leadership & Responsibility

Technical authority, release governance and quality accountability for AIG’s enterprise GenAI claims transformation

SITUATION · WHAT

Enterprise GenAI Claims Transformation

As GenAI Automation Architect at AIG, I lead Quality Engineering for Claim Assist, an enterprise GenAI transformation supporting UK Financial Lines, US Financial Lines and US Casualty across nine claim types. The platform operates in a highly regulated insurance environment and supports 17,000+ claims annually, with a broader claims landscape of more than 196,000 annual claims across the supported lines.

The program requires reliable AI validation, automated quality controls and disciplined release governance as functionality and transaction volumes scale.

TASK · WHY

Accountability for Technical Decisions and Production Quality

My responsibility extends beyond test execution. I lead an 18-member QA and automation team and am accountable for testing strategy, automation architecture, risk identification, release readiness, deployment coordination and production quality approval. BCS guidance defines Responsibility around autonomy, technical decisions, accountability and the impact of decisions; my role directly aligns with those areas.

I am entrusted with release-critical responsibilities because my quality assessments and decisions can affect production stability, claims operations, compliance and delivery timelines.

ACTION · HOW

Technical Authority and Release Gatekeeping

CI/CD Quality Gate

I participate directly in CI/CD deployment pipeline execution and release governance. I review test evidence, defect status and release-health conditions and provide the required QA/Data Quality approval before builds or configuration changes progress toward production.

Production Deployment Gatekeeper

I act as a quality gatekeeper for production deployments. I independently assess whether testing is complete, critical risks are addressed and release conditions are satisfied before giving QA sign-off. When conditions are not met, I communicate the risk and required mitigation rather than allowing the release to proceed.

Independent Risk Decisions

I determine defect severity and distinguish release-critical issues from items that can be deferred. I use structured red/yellow/green health indicators, defect assessments and mitigation plans to provide decision-ready release status to stakeholders.

Enterprise GenAI Quality

I designed automated sanity, regression and end-to-end validation across APIs, UI, integrated services and AI-driven workflows. I also govern human-in-the-loop validation of AI-generated outputs and document extraction across approximately 70–80+ fields.

My role is not simply to report whether testing passed. I exercise independent technical judgment on whether the evidence is sufficient for release, whether residual risks are acceptable and whether production promotion should receive QA approval.

The quality engineering and automation framework has supported improved release readiness, earlier identification of critical failures and greater confidence in production deployment. Claim Assist has reported a 60% reduction in claim-handling time, more than 360,000 annual efficiency hours and approximately $20–22 million in annual operational savings. The transformation is planned through 2030 with expansion across 21 countries.

This demonstrates responsibility for significant technical decisions, release governance, independent risk assessment, production sign-off and team leadership within a major enterprise GenAI program.
